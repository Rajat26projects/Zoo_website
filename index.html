Welcome to Indore Zoo 🦁

About Indore Zoo:

Our Mission: To promote wildlife conservation, educate visitors,and create awareness about protecting animals and nature.

Our Vision: To create a future where wildlife and people coexist peacefully and nature is protected.

Our History: Indore Zoo has been a place for wildlife lovers and visitors to learn about different animals and understand the importance of protecting wildlife and nature.

WildLife Conservation: We aim to spread awareness about wildlife conservation and encourage people to protect endangered animals, forests, and natural habitats.

Animal Care: We focus on providing animals with proper food, clean surroundings, medical care, and a safe environment for their well-being.

Visitor Experience: Our zoo provides visitors with an enjoyable and educational experience where they can observe wildlife, learn about animals, and spend quality time with family and friends.

Our Animals

Tigers 🐯

Tigers are powerful wild cats known for their strength, beauty, and unique stripes.

Lions 🦁

The lion is known as the king of the jungle. It is a powerful and social animal.

Bears 🐻

Bears are powerful mammals known for their strength and thick fur.

New Animals Arrival

Deers 🦌

Deer are gentle and graceful animals known for their speed and antlers. They mainly feed on grass, leaves, and plants.

Zebras 🦓

Zebras are wild animals famous for their unique black-and-white stripes. They mainly live in grasslands and feed on grass.

Aligators 🐊

Alligators are large reptiles known for their strong jaws and powerful bodies. They usually live in freshwater habitats.

    ⚠️ Visitor Guidelines:

    For the safety of visitors and animals
    please follow these rules:

    🚫 Do not feed the animals. It can be harmful to their health.
    🚫 Do not tease, disturb, or scare the animals.
    🚫 Do not throw stones, food, or other objects into the enclosures.
    🚧 Stay behind the safety barriers and never enter restricted areas.
    🔇 Avoid loud noises near animal enclosures.
    🗑️ Do not litter. Use the dustbins provided.
    📸 Do not use flash photography where it is restricted.
    👨‍👩‍👧 Keep children close and under supervision.
    🚭 Smoking and other prohibited activities are not allowed.
    ❤️ Respect wildlife and help us keep the zoo clean and safe.
